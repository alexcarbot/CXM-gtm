## Key Takeaways

### Customer Profile
- **Company**: MAP Automotive
- **Rooftops**: 10 (Lexington stores, Lake City Toyota, 3x Cookeville — Toyota/Kia/CDJR, Clinton Toyota, 2x Lexus Detroit)
- **Recent acquisitions**: 3 stores in Cookeville (acquired end of November)
- **Roles**: Charlie manages used cars/wholesale/acquisitions; Matt handles retail
- **Current CarBot usage**: Sarah (AI voice agent)

### Products Demonstrated
1. **CarBot Dealer website** — Managed solutions configurator (carbotdealer.com)
2. **CarBot Coach** — AI role-play training platform
3. **Carbon Motors demo** — Instant lead verification (sub-5-second outbound call)
4. **CarBot Cloud CXM** — Customer Experience Manager platform

### CXM-Specific Intelligence

**Chris's description**:
> "This is what we're preliminarily calling a CXM. It's meant to be a skin around any of your other services, notably your CRM or your DMS or your service scheduler to provide an AI native platform for all of your tools and systems to work together."

**Pricing**:
> "The CarBot Cloud is free for any CarBot dealer, period. Kind of hard stop."

**Open source**:
> "As a platform, we likely are going to come out with this open source next quarter."

**First deployment**: Installed at first dealership approximately one week prior to this call.

**Key capabilities discussed**:
- Calls in progress, history, conversations — all in one place
- Visual workflow editor: lead comes in → send text → send call → send email
- AI personas assignable to workflow steps
- AI watcher: auto-schedules appointments, enrolls confirmations, pushes back to CRM
- Nightly review of open activities/opportunities
- Auto-builds action items and tasks
- CRM integration: calls logged as calls, texts logged as notes (VIN Solutions limitation)

### Customer Reactions

**Charlie's response to CXM**:
> "That's great. That's what we need. Just to make sure that it's all in one house. All — you can see it all right there. I think that'll be very helpful."

**Interest in real-world validation**:
> "I'd be interested to see, outside of the demo, ones and twosies — what does it really look like after 21 days."

**Cookeville concern**:
> "They're a little bit salt of the earth kind of people. So I don't want to throw AI right into the mix."
