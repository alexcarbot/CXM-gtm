## Key Takeaways

### Customer Profile
- **Company**: BDC Pros (Lithia dealer services)
- **Context**: Evaluating BDC vendor partners via formal RFP process
- **Key stakeholders**: Marla Parial, Yasmine, Stephanie, JT (director — authored RFP metrics section)

### Products Demonstrated (4 SaaS products)
1. **CarBot Coach** — Role-play training platform
2. **Instant Lead Verification** — AI outbound first-response
3. **Live Streaming Dashboard** — Real-time call transcription and QA
4. **CarBot Cloud CXM** — Free AI-native dealer platform

### CarBot Coach Details
- 15-18 rooftops enrolled over 18 months
- 80% monthly active user adoption rate
- Built on customizable architecture: tracks, personas, scenarios
- Supports any training methodology (Joe Verde, Grant Cardone, custom)
- Persona examples: elderly non-tech-savvy customer vs. quick professional buyer
- Outbound or inbound call format — user role-plays with AI customer
- Gamification available: badges, streaks, tracks
- Technical: GPT-4o mini, custom 11 Labs voice, configurable temperature/tokens

### Instant Lead Verification Details
- Standalone first-response product
- Triggered via XML directly from source (faster) or CRM new-lead trigger
- AI agent (William) verifies identity, confirms lead interest
- Can soft-set appointment, live-transfer to salesperson, or offline with message
- Outbound from main dealership number or provisioned number
- Full compliance: 10DLC campaign registration, CNAM, STIR/SHAKEN, branded caller verification via TNS

### Live Streaming Dashboard
- Real-time transcription via media stream → websocket → transcription service
- Live listen capability (audio on computer before telephone due to upstream piping)
- Post-call: redacted transcription + redacted audio (PII beep-out)
- Smart PII redaction: distinguishes credit card numbers from employer addresses
- Powered by Assembly (medical-grade transcription)
- HIPAA-compliant architecture (proven via ClearMed, CarBot's healthcare AI company)

### CXM-Specific Intelligence

**Positioning**:
> "We won't call it a CRM, but it is a CRM and it will be open source."

**Pricing**:
> "Our CarBot Cloud CXM is free for any client that has any other CarBot product. There's not a charge for CarBot Cloud."

**Architecture**:
> "CarBot Cloud can be your CRM, it can lay on top of your eLead or your VIN or your DriveCentric or whatever other CRM you have. The goal here is to centralize as many services as possible."

**CRM integration specifics**:
- Calls logged as calls in VIN Solutions (not notes)
- Texts logged as notes (VIN Solutions hasn't opened messaging API yet — "3 years telling us")
- Emails: CarBot sends prettier-than-CRM emails with contextual micro-sites, posts link back to CRM
- Similar capability to Digital Air Strike / Quotable / ResponseLogic

**Workflow automation**:
> "Imagine if in eLead or in VIN, that workflow process you put together has a call, but instead of being a call task for a user, it's an AI agent outbound call."

**AI intelligence**:
- Auto-creates and auto-completes action items based on workflow
- Intelligently puts customers in buckets
- Campaign management (toggle on/off)

### Generational UI Strategy

**NADA insight**:
> "A lot of the UIs all had a pretty common theme — it seemed that theme was around their styling of being familiar to an inbox. Not just an email inbox, but typically an Outlook style or Gmail style inbox."

**Generational approach**:
> "What if we looked at UIs on a generational level? What if we looked at UIs from the lens of people of a different age, use different softwares and are familiar with different ecosystems?"

**~20 UI themes built**:
- Outlook style (traditional)
- Cleaner Apple style
- Discord
- Power BI dashboard
- Social media feed
- Native Gmail
- Native Outlook
- Apple Liquid Glass
- Salesforce
- TikTok feed
- "Zoidberg" (fun/internal)
- Live TV dashboard (for Apple TV / iOS devices)
