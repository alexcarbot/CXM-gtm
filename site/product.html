How It Works

Four Steps to One Screen.

🔗

1. Connect Your Systems

CXM integrates with your CRM and DMS. All customer, lead, and vehicle data flows in. Nothing migrates — CXM reads and writes to what you already have.

🛠️

2. Define Your Process

Use the visual workflow editor. Lead arrives → AI call → text on day 2 → email on day 3. Assign AI personas or human agents to each step.

🎨

3. Choose Your Interface

Pick from ~20 UI themes. Outlook for your GM, Discord for your BDC team, Power BI for analytics. Same data — different experience.

🚀

4. Let It Run

AI watchers monitor every interaction. Appointments auto-schedule. Tasks auto-complete. Every night, the system reviews every open opportunity.

FAQ

Common Questions.

Does CXM replace my CRM?

No. CXM sits on top of your CRM. All actions push back — calls logged as calls, tasks completed, appointments scheduled. Your CRM stays your system of record.

What does "free" mean?

Any dealer using any CarBot service gets CXM at no additional cost. No trial period, no feature gates, no per-seat charges. Paid value-add services (custom integrations, dedicated AI development) are available separately.

Which CRMs does it integrate with?

VIN Solutions, eLead, and DriveCentric are confirmed. Additional CRM integrations available through custom development.

Can different team members see different interfaces?

Yes. ~20 UI themes are available. Each user selects the interface that works for them. The underlying data and capabilities are identical across all themes.

How does AI know what to do after a call?

AI watchers analyze call transcripts in real time. Appointment set? Scheduled. Follow-up requested? Task created. Lead needs a new stage? Moved. Rules defined in your workflow — AI executes them.

Is my data secure?

Built on the same architecture as ClearMed, CarBot's HIPAA-compliant healthcare AI. PII redaction on all calls. 10DLC, CNAM, STIR/SHAKEN verification. SSO for enterprise deployment.

Can this run on a TV in the showroom?

Yes. CXM deploys natively to Apple TV and iOS devices. Put a live call dashboard on your sales floor TV or your manager's iPad.

What if we're not ready for AI?

Start with the communication view — all calls, texts, and emails in one place. The AI capabilities are there when you're ready, but CXM is valuable even as a pure visibility tool.
