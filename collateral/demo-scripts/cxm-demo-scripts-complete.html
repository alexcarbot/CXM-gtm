Pre-Demo Setup
Tabs: (1) Carbon Motors demo site (2) CXM dashboard. Phone face-up on table or visible on camera. Confirm telephony active.

Scene 1: The Hook

0:0045 sec
Open with a question — not a product name
Say This
"Quick question before I show you anything. When a lead comes in from your website at 9pm on a Tuesday night — what happens to it?"
Tip
Pause. Let them answer. Their answer becomes the thread you pull through the entire demo.
Transition
"Let me show you what happens with CXM. I'm going to do this live — no recordings, no slides. Just the real platform."

Scene 2: The 5-Second Call

0:451m 30s
The live "wow" — phone rings within 5 seconds
Say This
"I just submitted a lead on this dealership's website. Watch my phone. Count the seconds."
Action
Click "Submit." Hold phone up. Phone rings in ~5 seconds. Let the room react.
After Ring
"That was [X] seconds. AI knows my name, knows the vehicle, and is verifying my lead. This is what every web lead can look like — at 9am or 9pm, seven days a week."
Tip
Let AI speak for 10-15 seconds through phone speaker so the room hears the voice quality.
Bridge
"Every second of that call was captured in real time. Let me show you where it went."

Scene 3: The Dashboard

2:151m 15s
Real-time call intelligence
Action
Switch to CXM dashboard. Show the call with its live transcript.
Say This
"Full transcript, word by word, as it happened. Not a recording. Not a summary later. Real time. And if I'd clicked live-listen, I'd hear the audio on my computer before my phone — because we're upstream from the caller."
Scale It
"Imagine 15 calls across your store right now. Every one visible here — names, transcripts, departments. You'd know what's happening without leaving your chair."

Scene 4: The UI Switch

3:301m 30s
Same data, different feel — the generational differentiator
Set Up
"What app do you use the most on your phone? Outlook? Gmail?"
Action
Switch between 3 CXM themes: their preference → opposite generation → one more. Same customer record visible throughout.
Key Line
"Same customer. Same timeline. Same capabilities. Your GM sees Outlook. Your 23-year-old BDC rep sees Discord. Nobody learns something new — they use what they already know."
NADA Insight
"At NADA, every product looked like an email inbox. Because they're designed for the buyers, not the users. We built CXM for both."

Scene 5: The Close

5:001 min

"Everything I just showed you — the instant lead call, the live dashboard, the interface your team would actually want to use — is included free for any dealer on a CarBot service. No new contract. No new line item."
The Quote
"One of our dealer partners, running 10 rooftops, saw this and said: 'That's what we need. Just to make sure it's all in one house.' That's CXM. One platform. Your way."

Scene 6: Q&A Buffer

6:001 min
Engage and route
Open
"What questions do you have? And who else on your team should see this?"

"Replace our CRM?" → Sits on top. All actions push back.
"How is it free?" → Makes every CarBot service more valuable.
"What CRMs?" → VIN Solutions, eLead, DriveCentric.
"Real data?" → "Let's schedule a 15-min demo with your CRM connected."
Backup Plan
If live call fails: "The demo gods aren't cooperating — let me show you what this looks like when it works, which is every day for our live dealers." Navigate to a completed call.

Act II: The Platform

9 minutes  |  Scenes 4-7

5:003 min
Scene 4: The Workflow Builder
Transition
"That instant call is powerful — but it's just step one. Let me show you what happens next."
Action
Navigate to workflow editor. Show a pre-built sales BDC workflow.
Walk-Through
"Lead comes in. Step one: AI verification call — that's what you just saw. Step two: text on day two. Step three: email on day three with the vehicle. Step four: AI follow-up on day five. Step five: human callback."
Differentiator
"Notice — some steps are AI, some are human. You decide. Your CRM can't put an AI agent into a workflow step. CXM can."
Landing
"Build it once. It runs for every lead, every time. No missed steps. No forgotten callbacks."
8:002m 30s
Scene 5: The AI Watcher
Action
Navigate to a completed call that included an appointment set on the call.
Story
"This call happened earlier. Customer said yes to 2pm tomorrow. Watch what happened next — without anyone touching anything."
Visual
Show cascade: (1) Appointment in CRM (2) Confirmation text sent (3) Task completed (4) Lead stage advanced.
Punchline
"AI reviewed the transcript, identified the appointment, scheduled it, confirmed it, and marked the task complete. Nobody did anything. And tonight it'll review every open opportunity and ask: did anything get missed?"
Contrast
"Most AI tools push a note into your CRM. A note. CXM doesn't push notes — it completes actions."
10:302m 30s
Scene 6: The UI Switch — Generational Design
Set Up
"I want to show you something from an observation we made at NADA. Every product on the floor had the same UI — an email inbox. Because those products are designed for the people who buy them. But the people who use them live on Discord and TikTok."
The Question
"What if the same platform looked different to different people?"
Action
Switch CXM theme live: Outlook → Discord → Social Feed → Power BI → TikTok. Same customer record visible. Let the visual impact land.
Key Line
"Same customer, same data. About 20 themes — three generations. Your seasoned operators get what they know. Your digital natives get what they want. Nobody learns something new."
Engagement
Ask: "Which one would your team want?" This is when people lean forward. Let them talk.
13:001 min
Scene 7: Compliance (Brief)
If IT Present
"Quick architecture note: 10DLC, CNAM, STIR/SHAKEN standard. Context-aware PII redaction. Built for HIPAA. SSO supported."
If IT Not Present
"The architecture was built for HIPAA healthcare. Automotive compliance is a subset. We have a 30-minute technical session when your IT team is ready."
