Product Definition

What Is CarBot Cloud CXM?

A free, AI-native platform that sits on top of your existing CRM and DMS to unify all dealer communications and services in one place — in the interface each team member already knows.

🏠

One House

Every call, text, email, and AI conversation in a single timeline per customer.

🎨

Your Interface

~20 UI themes designed for how people actually work. Outlook for GMs, Discord for BDC.

🤖

AI That Watches

After every call, AI schedules appointments, completes tasks, and updates your CRM.

Audience Strategy

Two Audiences. One Platform.

"I manage the operation"

GMs, Operating Partners, seasoned staff

  • Cautious about AI — want familiarity first
  • UI: Outlook, Gmail, Apple Mail
  • Lead with consolidation, visibility, simplicity
  • Avoid AI-forward language, disruption framing
"I don't want to throw AI right into the mix."
— Charles Lovell, MAP Automotive

"I work the leads"

BDC agents, newer salespeople, digital-native managers

  • Expect modern UX — want to explore
  • UI: Discord, TikTok, Apple Liquid Glass
  • Lead with speed, modern interface, customization
  • Avoid legacy framing, over-explaining basics
"Products are built for buyers, not users."
— NADA observation, Chris Sondesky
