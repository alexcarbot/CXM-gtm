CXM Sales Training Guide

Everything you need to position, demo, and close CarBot Cloud CXM.

01 What Is CXM?

One-Sentence Positioning

CarBot Cloud CXM is the free, AI-native platform that unifies every dealer communication and service in the interface each team member already knows.

Key Facts

  • Price: Free for any dealer using any CarBot service
  • What it does: Sits on top of existing CRM/DMS, unifies all communications, adds AI workflow automation
  • What it is NOT: A CRM replacement. It's a CRM overlay.
  • Differentiator: ~20 UI themes across 3 generations + AI that executes workflow steps (not just notes)
  • Heritage: 8-9 years of automotive AI, architecture proven in HIPAA healthcare

03 Talk Tracks

GM — 20 Seconds

"CarBot Cloud CXM puts every call, text, email, and AI conversation across your whole operation into one screen. AI handles the follow-through — scheduling appointments, confirming them, updating your CRM. It's free for CarBot clients, and it looks like the tools your team already uses."

GM — 60 Seconds

"You're running multiple rooftops. Right now, to know what happened with a customer, someone has to pull up the CRM, check call logs, look at texts, and maybe check an AI tool that pushed a note somewhere. CXM puts all of that in one timeline per customer. When a call ends and an appointment is set, AI schedules it, sends the confirmation, and marks the task complete — no one has to touch it. Your team uses a familiar interface — Outlook, Gmail, Apple Mail — so there's nothing new to learn. And it's included free with your CarBot services."

GSM — 20 Seconds

"CXM gives your team one place to see every lead, every call, and every follow-up — with AI that doesn't just log notes but actually schedules appointments and completes tasks. Your reps pick the interface they want, and it's free."

GSM — 60 Seconds

"Your BDC process is only as good as the people executing it — and people miss steps. CXM fixes that. You build your follow-up process once in a visual editor: AI calls the lead in 5 seconds, sends a text on day two, emails on day three, follows up on day five. AI handles the routine. Your team handles the closeable ups. When a call ends and the customer sets an appointment, nobody does anything. The system schedules it, confirms it, and marks the task complete."

IT — 20 Seconds

"CXM integrates with your CRM via API — calls logged as calls, tasks pushed back on completion. 10DLC, CNAM, STIR/SHAKEN standard. PII redaction on all transcripts. SSO supported. The architecture was built for HIPAA."

04 Demo Playbook

Three demo paths based on time and audience. Every demo starts with the same question:

Opening Line

"Before I show you anything, I want to understand what matters most to you. What's the biggest gap you're trying to fill right now?"

7-Minute Lightning Demo (GMs, conferences)

  1. The 5-Second Call (2 min) — Submit lead on demo site. Phone rings. AI agent engages.
  2. The Live Dashboard (2 min) — Show call appearing with real-time transcription.
  3. The UI Switch (2 min) — Same data: Outlook → Discord → social feed.
  4. Close (1 min) — "This is free for CarBot clients. What questions do you have?"

15-Minute Standard Demo (GSMs, BDC managers)

  1. The 5-Second Call (2 min)
  2. The Live Dashboard (2 min)
  3. The Workflow (4 min) — Walk through visual workflow editor with AI + human steps.
  4. The AI Watcher (3 min) — Show auto-scheduling, auto-task completion after call.
  5. The UI Switch (2 min)
  6. Close (2 min)

30-Minute Deep Dive (IT, enterprise procurement)

  1. The 5-Second Call with trigger mechanism explanation (3 min)
  2. The Live Dashboard with live listen and QA scoring (3 min)
  3. CRM Integration — data flowing back to CRM (5 min)
  4. The Workflow — deep dive with branching logic (5 min)
  5. AI Configuration — persona setup, model, voice, prompting (3 min)
  6. The AI Watcher (3 min)
  7. Compliance — PII redaction, 10DLC, STIR/SHAKEN (3 min)
  8. The UI Switch (2 min)
  9. Close (3 min)

05 Objection Handling

They SayYou Say
"We already have a CRM." CXM doesn't replace it. It sits on top. Think of it as the windshield, not the engine.
"We're using Impel." Impel pushes notes. Does it schedule the appointment after the call? CXM follows through.
"How is this free?" CXM makes every other CarBot service more valuable. When everything's in one place, you use more of what you already pay for.
"My team won't use it." That's why we built 20 interfaces. Your GM gets Outlook. Your BDC rep gets Discord. Nobody learns something new.
"We don't trust AI." You define every word, voice, and handoff rule. Every call streams live. You can listen and take over anytime.
"We just acquired new stores." CXM is how you manage them remotely. Start with the Outlook view. Layer in AI when they're ready.
"What about compliance?" 10DLC, CNAM, STIR/SHAKEN — all standard. PII redaction on every transcript. Architecture built for HIPAA.

06 Discovery Questions

Opening

  1. "How many systems does your team switch between to work a single lead?"
  2. "When a lead comes in at 9pm, what happens?"
  3. "How do you see what's happening across all locations in real time?"

Pain

  1. "What falls through the cracks most — first response, follow-up, or confirmation?"
  2. "How many of your AI tools push notes versus actually completing tasks?"
  3. "What happens to your process when your best BDC rep calls in sick?"

Impact

  1. "If every lead got contacted in 5 seconds and every appointment got confirmed automatically, what would that be worth?"
  2. "How much time does your team spend on admin that AI could handle?"

Decision

  1. "Who else needs to be involved in evaluating this?"
  2. "What's your timeline for dealer technology decisions this year?"

07 What NOT to Say

Off-Limits

  • Do NOT mention open-source plans (internal only — Tier C)
  • Do NOT promise specific ROI numbers (no production data yet)
  • Do NOT call CXM a "CRM replacement" — always "CRM overlay" or "sits on top of"
  • Do NOT name customer quotes by name — use anonymous ("dealer group, 10 rooftops")
  • Do NOT make competitive claims about Impel/Car Wars without the qualifying question format
  • Do NOT use: "revolutionary," "game-changing," "seamless," "AI-powered," "guaranteed"
