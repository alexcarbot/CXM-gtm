When They Say / You Say

"We already have a CRM."
CXM doesn't replace it. It sits on top. Think of it as the windshield, not the engine. All actions push back to your CRM.
"We're already using Impel."
Impel pushes notes. Does it schedule the appointment after the call? Does it complete the task? CXM follows through.
"How is this free?"
CXM makes every other CarBot service more valuable. When everything lives in one place, you use more of what you already pay for.
"My team won't use another tool."
That's why we built 20 interfaces. Your GM gets Outlook. Your 23-year-old BDC rep gets Discord. Nobody learns something new.
"We don't trust AI with customers."
You define every word, voice, and handoff rule. Every call streams live — you can listen and take over anytime. Not a black box.
"We just acquired new stores."
CXM is how you manage them remotely. Start with Outlook view — familiar. Layer in AI when they're ready. No disruption.

Landmines to Plant

  1. "Can your AI agents execute workflow steps, or do they just generate notes?" — Most can't.
  2. "Can different team members see different interfaces for the same data?" — Nobody else does this.
  3. "Show me a PII-redacted transcript that still has useful context." — Most redaction is blunt.
  4. "What happens after a call ends? Does anything update automatically?" — CXM's AI watcher does.

Walk Away If

  • No existing CarBot service and doesn't want one (CXM is free with services only)
  • Mid-CRM migration — won't stabilize for 6+ months
  • IT blocks all third-party API access
  • Demands guaranteed ROI numbers (no production data yet)
