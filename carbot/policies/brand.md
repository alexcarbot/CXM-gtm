# CarBot Brand Policy

## Brand Foundation

CarBot's positioning posture (applies to ALL materials):

### Heritage
> We built automotive AI before it was a category.

- CarBot has deep roots in automotive
- Not a generic AI adapted for dealers
- Years of automotive-specific development
- Evidence: founding story, product history

### Depth
> Purpose-built for automotive, not adapted from generic solutions.

- Every feature designed for dealership workflows
- Understands DMS, CRM, BDC, service lane
- Speaks dealer vocabulary natively
- Evidence: integration depth, feature specificity

### Compliance-First
> Enterprise-grade TCPA/CASL architecture from day one.

- Built with compliance as foundation, not afterthought
- Mechanisms for consent, opt-out, quiet hours, audit
- Not just features—architectural decisions
- Evidence: compliance features in codebase

### Premium Positioning
> The platform serious operators choose.

- Not the cheapest, not trying to be
- For dealers who want it done right
- Enterprise-grade for growing groups
- Evidence: customer profile, feature depth

## Voice & Tone

### Core Attributes

| Attribute | Description | Example |
|-----------|-------------|---------|
| **Confident** | We know what we're doing | "Built for automotive" not "Can work for automotive" |
| **Calm** | No hype, no urgency tricks | "When you're ready" not "Limited time!" |
| **Specific** | Concrete over vague | "Handles quiet hours" not "Compliance features" |
| **Direct** | Get to the point | Short sentences, clear benefits |
| **Warm** | Automotive is relationships | "Your team" not "Users" |

### What We Sound Like

**Good:**
- "CarBot captures every lead, even at 2am."
- "Your BDC team gets backup that never calls in sick."
- "TCPA compliance isn't an add-on—it's how we're built."

**Bad:**
- "Revolutionary AI-powered solution that leverages cutting-edge technology..."
- "Don't miss out on this game-changing opportunity!"
- "We're the #1 rated automotive AI platform!" (unless proven)

### Tone by Audience

**GM / Operating Partners:**
- Business outcome focused
- ROI and efficiency language
- Risk reduction emphasis
- "Your operation" framing

**General Sales Managers:**
- Performance and speed focus
- Lead and conversion language
- Team empowerment
- "Your sales floor" framing

**IT / Enterprise:**
- Technical precision
- Integration and security focus
- No fluff, just facts
- "Your infrastructure" framing

## Language Guidelines

### Approved Vocabulary

| Use This | Not This |
|----------|----------|
| Conversation | Call handling |
| Lead capture | Lead generation |
| Follow-up automation | Drip campaigns |
| Compliance mechanisms | Compliance guaranteed |
| Enterprise-grade | Best-in-class |
| Purpose-built | AI-powered |
| Automotive-specific | Industry-leading |
| Designed to | Guaranteed to |

### Words to Avoid

| Avoid | Why |
|-------|-----|
| Revolutionary | Overused, unprovable |
| Game-changing | Hype language |
| Best-in-class | Requires proof |
| Industry-leading | Requires proof |
| Cutting-edge | Vague, overused |
| Seamless | Usually not true |
| Turnkey | Oversimplifies |
| Guaranteed | Legal risk |
| AI-powered | Everyone says this |
| Leverage | Corporate jargon |

### Competitor References

**Do:**
- "Ask your current provider how they handle..."
- "Questions to ask any vendor..."
- "Typical approaches vs. CarBot's approach"

**Don't:**
- "[Competitor] can't do this"
- "[Competitor] has poor compliance"
- Direct feature-by-feature attacks

## Visual & Format Guidelines

### Headlines
- 7 words or fewer
- Benefit-first
- No punctuation tricks (!!! or ALL CAPS)
- One idea per headline

### Body Copy
- Short paragraphs (2-3 sentences)
- Bullet points for features
- Active voice
- Present tense for capabilities

### CTAs
- Clear, specific action
- Low-commitment options for researchers
- "Schedule a demo" not "Get started free"
- "See how it works" not "Learn more"

## Brand Don'ts

### Never Do
- Claim certifications we don't have
- Use customer logos without permission
- Quote customers without approval
- Promise specific ROI numbers
- Compare directly to competitors without evidence
- Use fear-based messaging
- Exaggerate capabilities

### Avoid
- Exclamation points in body copy
- Questions as headlines (usually)
- Generic stock photos
- "We're excited to announce..."
- Emoji in professional materials
- Buzzword-heavy sentences

## Compliance with Brand

All GTM materials must:
1. Align with Brand Foundation pillars
2. Use approved vocabulary
3. Match tone for target audience
4. Avoid prohibited language
5. Have evidence for any claims

## Brand Review

For materials going external:
- Website copy: Marketing review
- Sales materials: Sales leadership review
- Press/PR: Executive review
- Legal-adjacent claims: Legal review
