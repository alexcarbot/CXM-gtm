# GTM Compliance Policy

## Purpose

This policy ensures GTM materials do not create legal, regulatory, or competitive risk for CarBot.

## Scope

Applies to all GTM outputs:
- Website copy
- Sales materials
- Demo scripts
- Marketing collateral
- Email templates
- Social media content

## Regulatory Considerations

### TCPA (Telephone Consumer Protection Act)

**What We Can Say:**
- "CarBot includes mechanisms for TCPA compliance"
- "Built-in consent capture and opt-out handling"
- "Quiet hours enforcement across time zones"
- "Audit trails for compliance review"

**What We Cannot Say:**
- "TCPA compliant" (implies guarantee)
- "Ensures TCPA compliance" (promises outcome)
- "Never get fined" (unverifiable)
- "100% compliant" (absolute claims)

**Safe Phrasing:**
> "CarBot is designed with TCPA compliance in mind, including consent management, opt-out handling, and quiet hours enforcement."

### CASL (Canada's Anti-Spam Legislation)

Same principles as TCPA:
- Describe mechanisms, not outcomes
- No compliance guarantees
- Reference features, not promises

### Data Privacy (GDPR/CCPA)

**What We Can Say:**
- "Data handling controls"
- "Retention management features"
- "Access controls and audit logging"
- "Designed with privacy in mind"

**What We Cannot Say:**
- "GDPR compliant" (without certification)
- "CCPA certified" (no such thing)
- "Your data is safe" (absolute claim)

## Certification & Compliance Claims

### Rules

1. **Only claim certifications we hold**
   - If we have SOC 2, say SOC 2
   - If pending, say "pursuing" not "compliant"

2. **No implied certifications**
   - "Enterprise security" ≠ SOC 2
   - "Bank-level encryption" needs evidence

3. **Date certifications**
   - Certifications expire
   - Include "as of [date]" when relevant

### Currently Verifiable
- List actual certifications here
- Update as obtained

### In Progress
- List pending certifications
- Can say "pursuing" or "in process"

## Competitive Claims

### Absolutely Prohibited
- False statements about competitors
- Unverifiable competitor weaknesses
- Using competitor customer data
- Trademark misuse

### Allowed with Evidence
- Publicly available competitor information
- Published competitor limitations
- Documented feature differences

### Safe Alternative: Questions
Instead of claims, use questions:

❌ "Unlike [Competitor], we have real compliance"

✅ "Questions to ask any vendor:
- How do you handle consent management?
- Can I see your audit log?
- What happens during a compliance review?"

## Performance Claims

### Prohibited Without Evidence
- Response time claims (e.g., "sub-second")
- Uptime percentages (e.g., "99.9%")
- ROI numbers (e.g., "300% ROI")
- Cost savings (e.g., "Save $50K/year")

### Allowed Phrasing
- "Designed for fast response"
- "Built for reliability"
- "Customers report improved efficiency"
- "Typical results vary by deployment"

### With Customer Permission
- Named case studies with approval
- Specific metrics with sign-off
- Testimonials with written consent

## Testimonials & Social Proof

### Requirements
1. Written permission from customer
2. Accurate quote (no embellishment)
3. Current relationship (not churned)
4. Approved use case (where it will appear)

### Placeholder Language
When we don't have testimonials:
- "[Customer testimonial placeholder - awaiting approval]"
- "See what dealers are saying" → link to request

### Never
- Fake testimonials
- AI-generated quotes
- Composite customers
- Outdated quotes from churned customers

## Integration Claims

### Can Claim
- Integrations we have built and tested
- Integrations with documentation
- Partner integrations with agreement

### Cannot Claim
- "Integrates with everything"
- Integrations not yet built
- Partner relationships without agreement

### Safe Phrasing
- "Integrates with major DMS platforms including..."
- "CRM connectivity via API"
- "Ask about your specific systems"

## Legal Review Triggers

Send to legal review when:
- Claiming certifications or compliance
- Using customer names or quotes
- Making competitor comparisons
- Promising specific outcomes
- Contract or pricing language
- Terms of service references

## Risk Classification

### Low Risk (Self-approve)
- Feature descriptions from codebase
- Process explanations
- Generic benefits without metrics

### Medium Risk (Peer Review)
- Outcome claims with qualifiers
- Integration references
- Audience-specific messaging

### High Risk (Legal Review)
- Compliance language
- Competitive positioning
- Customer references
- Performance metrics

## Documentation

All compliance decisions must be documented:
1. What was claimed
2. What evidence supports it
3. What tier it's approved for
4. Who approved it
5. When it expires (if applicable)
