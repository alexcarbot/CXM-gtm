7 Minutes  |  In-Person or Virtual

"The Lightning Demo"

A high-impact 7-minute presentation designed for conferences, first meetings, or time-constrained executives. Combines live demo moments with commercial storytelling. Works in-person or virtual.

SceneNameDurationPurpose
1The Hook0:45Set the problem, create urgency
2The 5-Second Call1:30"Wow" moment — live AI lead response
3The Dashboard1:15Show real-time visibility

6Q&A Buffer1:00Engagement and next steps

Pre-Demo Setup

Stage Direction
Before the presentation: Have Carbon Motors demo site open in a browser tab. Have CXM dashboard open in a second tab. Have your phone face-up on the table or visible on camera. Confirm telephony is active with a test call.

Scene 1: The Hook

0:00 45 sec
Open with a question — not a product name
Say This
"Quick question before I show you anything. When a lead comes in from your website at 9pm on a Tuesday night — what happens to it?"
Presenter Tip
Pause. Let them answer. Common responses: "It waits until morning," "Hopefully someone checks," "Our AI sends a text." Each response sets up what you're about to show.
Transition
"Let me show you what happens with CXM. I'm going to do this live — no recordings, no slides. Just the real platform."
Visual Cue
Navigate to Carbon Motors demo website. Fill in the lead form with your real phone number. Pause before clicking submit.

Scene 2: The 5-Second Call

0:45 1 min 30s
The live "wow" moment — phone rings within 5 seconds
Say This
"I just submitted a lead on this dealership's website. Watch my phone. Count the seconds."
Stage Direction
Click "Submit." Hold phone up (in-person) or position camera (virtual). The phone should ring within 5 seconds. Let the room react.
After Phone Rings
"That was [X] seconds. The AI knows my name, knows the vehicle I asked about, and is verifying my lead right now. This is what every web lead can look like — at 9am or 9pm, seven days a week."
Presenter Tip
Let the AI agent speak for 10-15 seconds through the phone speaker so the room hears the voice quality. Then end the call.
Bridge to Dashboard
"Now — every second of that call was captured in real time. Let me show you where it went."

Scene 3: The Dashboard

2:15 1 min 15s
Show where the call landed — real-time intelligence
Stage Direction
Switch to the CXM dashboard tab. The call that just happened should be visible with its transcription.
Say This
"Here's that call. Full transcript, word by word, as it happened — not a recording you listen to later. And if I'd clicked this live-listen button during the call, I would have heard the audio on my computer before I heard it on my phone. Because we're upstream from the caller."
Feature Highlight
"Right now, if you had 15 calls happening across your store, every single one would be visible here — live. Names, transcriptions, departments. You'd know exactly what's happening on your floor without leaving your office. Or your home."
If Audience Is a GM
Emphasize: "This is how you manage Cookeville from Lexington." Reference the remote management use case.

Scene 4: The UI Switch

3:30 1 min 30s
The generational differentiator — same data, different feel
Set Up
"Quick question — what app do you use the most on your phone? Outlook? Gmail? Something else?"
Presenter Tip
Listen to their answer. Show that theme first. Then switch to the opposite generation.
Stage Direction
Switch between 3 CXM themes: their preference → opposite generation → one more. Keep the same customer record visible so they can see it's identical data.
Key Line
"Same customer. Same timeline. Same capabilities. But your GM sees Outlook. Your 23-year-old BDC rep sees Discord. Your sales floor TV shows a live feed. Nobody learns something new — they use what they already know."
The NADA Insight
"At NADA this year, every product in the hall looked like an email inbox. Because they're designed for the people who buy them — not the people who use them. We built CXM for both."

Scene 5: The One-Line Close

5:00 1 min
Land the positioning and open the door
The Close
"Everything I just showed you — the instant lead call, the live dashboard, the interface your team would actually want to use — is included free for any dealer on a CarBot service. No new contract. No new line item. Your CarBot rep sets it up."
Pause, Then
"One of our dealer partners, a group running 10 rooftops, saw this and said: 'That's what we need. Just to make sure it's all in one house.' That's CXM. One platform. Your way."
Do Not
Do not rush into pricing details, paid tiers, or technical architecture here. Keep it clean. Let them ask.

Scene 6: Q&A Buffer

6:00 1 min
Engage and route to next step
Open
"What questions do you have? And who else on your team should see this?"
Common Questions → Answers
"Does this replace our CRM?" → "No. Sits on top. All actions push back."
"How is it free?" → "It makes every other CarBot service more valuable."
"What CRMs do you integrate with?" → "VIN Solutions, eLead, DriveCentric."
"Can I see it with real data?" → "Absolutely. Let's schedule a 15-minute demo with your CRM connected."
Next Step CTA
If interested: "I can get your CarBot rep to set up a CXM instance with your CRM this week."
If more time needed: "Let me send you the one-pager and schedule a 15-minute deeper dive with your team."

Backup Plan

If the Live Call Fails
"The demo gods aren't cooperating — but let me show you what this looks like when it works, which is every day for our live dealers." Navigate to a pre-completed call in the CXM dashboard and walk through the transcript + automation cascade.
