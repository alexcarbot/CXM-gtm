:60 Commercial — "9:15 PM"

A storytelling narrative that follows a single lead from submission to sold — showing how CXM works when nobody's watching.

FormatDurationAudienceUse
Video / Audio / Conference Reel60 secondsGMs, GSMs, Decision-MakersTrade shows, social media, website hero, pre-demo warmup

The Script

Narration (V.O.)
0:00"It's 9:15 on a Tuesday night. Your showroom is dark. Your team went home two hours ago."
Visual
Empty dealership lot. Lights off. One car under a streetlight. A phone screen glows — someone browsing a dealer website from their couch.
Narration (V.O.)
0:07"But a customer just found the car they want. They submitted a lead. And in five seconds..."
Visual
Close-up: phone rings. Caller ID shows the dealership name. Customer looks surprised — picks up.
AI Voice (William)
0:13"Hi, this is William at [Dealership]. I see you were looking at the 2024 CR-V. Great choice — are you available to come see it tomorrow?"
Narration (V.O.)
0:21"The customer says yes. And without a single person touching a keyboard..."
Visual
Split screen animation — CXM dashboard: appointment auto-populates. CRM updates. Confirmation text sends. Task marks complete. All happening simultaneously, smoothly.
Narration (V.O.)
0:28"...the appointment is scheduled. The CRM is updated. A confirmation goes out. The task is done."
Visual
Morning. Dealership opens. Salesperson walks in, opens CXM on their phone — sees the full conversation timeline. Smiles.
Narration (V.O.)
0:34"When your salesperson walks in the next morning, they already know the customer's name, the car they want, and the conversation that happened. They just have to shake their hand."
Visual
Quick montage: CXM in Outlook view (GM watching from office), Discord view (young BDC rep texting), social feed view (sales floor TV). All showing the same data.
Narration (V.O.)
0:44"CarBot Cloud CXM. Every call, every lead, every follow-up — in the interface your team already knows. Free for CarBot clients."
Visual / End Card
0:54CarBot logo. Tagline: "One Platform. Your Way." CTA: "Schedule a Demo" with URL.

Key Messaging Embedded

MessageMomentClaims Tier
Sub-5-second lead response0:07 — "in five seconds"Tier A
AI follows through (not just notes)0:21 — "without a single person touching a keyboard"Tier A
Auto-scheduling, CRM update, task completion0:28 — appointment/CRM/confirmation/taskTier B (sales OK)
Generational UI0:44 — Outlook/Discord/social montageTier A
Free for CarBot clients0:44 — "Free for CarBot clients"Tier A

Production Notes

Tone: Calm, confident, warm. Not hype — just showing what happens. Let the speed and automation speak for itself.

Music: Minimal ambient. Build slightly at 0:28 when the automation cascade happens. Resolve at the end card.

Pacing: Slow at the open (empty lot), quick at the cascade (0:21-0:34), steady at the close.

Alternative cuts: :30 version (cut 0:34-0:44, go straight from cascade to end card). :15 version (just 0:07-0:21 — the call moment).

Commercial Script
60 Seconds  |  Video

:60 Commercial — "Same Data. Their Way."

A visual-first spot built around the generational UI differentiator. Designed for social media and conference reels.

FormatDurationAudienceUse
Video (social-optimized: 9:16 + 16:9)60 secondsBoth audiences — bridges the generational gapSocial media, conference screens, website product section

The Script

Visual
0:00A dealership GM, mid-50s, sits at his desk. He opens CXM — it looks exactly like Outlook. He nods approvingly. Familiar. Comfortable.
Narration (V.O.)
0:05"Mike's been running dealerships for 25 years. He likes what he knows."
Visual
0:10Cut to: a BDC rep, early 20s, at a standing desk. She opens CXM — it looks like Discord. Dark mode, chat threads, real-time notifications pinging. She's in her element.
Narration (V.O.)
0:15"Jess started six months ago. She wants tools that move as fast as she does."
Visual
0:20Split screen: Mike on the left (Outlook view), Jess on the right (Discord view). The SAME customer record is visible on both screens — same name, same timeline, same data. Different interface.
Narration (V.O.)
0:22"Same customer. Same data. Same platform."
Visual
0:26Rapid-fire montage of CXM themes: Power BI dashboard on a wall TV. TikTok feed on a phone. Salesforce view on a laptop. Apple Liquid Glass on an iPad. Social feed on an Apple TV in the showroom. Each showing the same live call data.
Narration (V.O.)
0:30"Twenty interfaces. Three generations. One platform."
Visual
0:34Mike sees a live call come in. Jess picks it up. The live transcription scrolls on Mike's Outlook screen as Jess talks. Mike watches from his office — he can see everything.
Narration (V.O.)
0:38"At NADA, every product looked like an email inbox — because they're designed for the people who buy them, not the people who use them."
Visual
0:45Jess and Mike both look up and nod at each other across the showroom floor. Small moment of connection.
Narration (V.O.)
0:47"We built CarBot Cloud CXM for both of them."
Visual / End Card
0:52CarBot logo. "One Platform. Your Way." Free for CarBot clients. Schedule a Demo.

Key Messaging Embedded

MessageMoment
Generational UI — same data, different experienceCore concept — runs through entire spot
Built for users, not just buyers0:38 — NADA observation
Real-time call visibility0:34 — Mike watching Jess's live call
~20 themes0:26-0:30 — rapid-fire montage
Free for CarBot clients0:52 — end card

Production Notes

Tone: Human, warm, generational bridge. Not "old vs. young" — it's "both work best their own way."

Music: Something that subtly bridges eras — start with a classic feel, morph into something more modern at 0:10, blend at 0:22.

Social cuts: :30 (just the split screen + montage + end card). :15 (Mike/Jess split + "Same data. Their way." + logo).
